About

Research meets systems.

I am a Research Engineer at MedOS Tech in Abu Dhabi, working on AI for clinical decision support, medical research, and workflow automation. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning research and practical system building — especially LLMs, agentic AI, and world models for scientific and medical settings.

Before MedOS Tech I was an ML Intern at the Materials Informatics Lab, IIT Madras, on optimization-driven predictive modeling for materials science, and a Research Intern at DoMS, IIT Madras, on structured reasoning and applied ML systems. I am completing a B.S. in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras.

I care about models that are useful outside the notebook: clear evaluation, reliable orchestration, and systems you can actually put next to real workflows.
